As you might see, I had a relatively positive on generative AI for potential discovery on mathematical results and I do not hide myself that I used generative AI (Gemini and Claude) sometimes to get some intuitions or background study, or simulation generation for better presentation. Mostly, I used generative AI so far is to 

1. prepare teaching materials to provide better learning experiences (historical survey);
2. get some structural first feedback on essay;
3. understand basic research languages on different research areas;
4. understand the nuance on English expression (break language barrier);
5. feedback on my thoughts;
6. some discovery on mathematics related to my area.

There were good reasons so far to use generative AI as a researcher to expand my knowledge outside my realm, but I gradually changed my mind when I read OpenAI announcement on the 'resolution' of the 10 problems and kept reading arXiv posts on declaration of AI usage in their preprints.
It is too awkward for me to see why we promote AI models. That starts change on my mind, and the problem is that it gradually breakdowns mathematics community. Currently, we are in flood of mathematics papers and the journal system is breaking down.

There were several attempts that I used generative AI to find potential approach to solve a problem before, and some of them were good and I liked it before to get potential new discovery such as AlphaEvolve and others. However, after I read these articles, I think we need to think about the mathematics community more. 

- [Tasmin Chu, Mathematicians need to act](https://tasmin.substack.com/p/mathematicians-need-to-act?utm_campaign=post-expanded-share&utm_medium=web)
- [Max Weinreich, the crisis of AI-generated mathematics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02859)

We might need to discuss this matter more seriously. Education system is breaking down and journal system is breaking down. I believe that those are not good for humanities. 

I have used generative AI in multiple perspective: schedule controller, data collection, generating numerical simulation, coding problems, literature survey from different discipline, some historical text that I cannot read an article, say French. All of those are for my convenience to track potential mistakes and for delivering key ideas on mathematics to broad audience better. 

However, I think we are facing with lots of danger for our community safety. From now on, I will not use generative AI for my mathematics research unless we find a way to keep mathematics community vibrant.
